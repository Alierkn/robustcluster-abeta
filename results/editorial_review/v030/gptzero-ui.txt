							50 container Molecular simulation data require both statistical analysis and an understanding of how the observations were generated. This curricular internship used amyloid-β40 descriptors to connect practical work with GROMACS, VMD and Python to a reproducible analysis of conformational clustering. The report develops the scientific background, examines the supplied implementation of the workflow of Cadenelli and colleagues, and presents a separate experiment on the choice of clustering features.




The available table contains 300,003 observations from three replicas over the source-labelled 1–3 microsecond interval. The audit identified problems in input handling, residual-cluster treatment, free-energy interpolation and weighted summaries. A modular implementation addresses these operations through explicit validation, numerical tests and recorded run settings. The recovered free-energy surface contains the complete grid and covers all converted descriptor queries. Its energy units and construction procedure remain unverified, so equilibrium clustering and population estimates are not reported.




The completed descriptor analysis found a pooled correlation of 0.848 between radius of gyration and solvent-accessible surface area (SASA). The first two standardized principal components account for 88.1% of variance. To examine whether the correlation justified removing SASA, Gaussian mixtures were fitted to four and three descriptors using one shared, replica-balanced fitting sample. Five component counts and five initialization seeds produced fifty converged fits. The median agreement between feature sets was 0.864 at three components and 0.457 at six, showing that the effect of feature removal depends on the fitted model.
