# Service limits reviewed before submission

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Official service documentation is the source; actual browser gates take precedence over advertised availability.

| Service | Published free limit | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sapling | 2,000 characters per check; longer text can be truncated | [Pricing](https://sapling.ai/pricing); [detector FAQ](https://sapling.ai/ai-content-detector) |
| GPTZero | Free plan: 10,000 characters per scan and 10,000 words per month; account-free availability is not guaranteed | [Help Center, updated 19 October 2026](https://support.gptzero.me/articles/1272562776-what-is-the-difference-between-the-free-and-paid-for-planspricing) |
| QuillBot | Up to 1,200 words per free scan and 6 scans per day; explanatory text recommends/requires 80 words, although input placeholder says 40 | [Official detector](https://quillbot.com/ai-content-detector); [Help Center](https://help.quillbot.com/hc/en-us/articles/35295733817111-Is-Quillbot-s-AI-Detector-free-or-premium) |

The common 2,000-character ceiling determines the excerpt scope. The full main text exceeds this ceiling. No complete-report classification will be claimed. GPTZero previously presented a sign-up gate and a stale background score; any renewed gate will be logged as BLOCKED. QuillBot offers rewriting and humanization controls elsewhere in its interface; those functions are outside this review and will not be used.

Privacy references consulted: [Sapling](https://sapling.ai/privacy), [GPTZero](https://gptzero.me/privacy-policy.html), [QuillBot](https://quillbot.com/privacy). These are external submissions, not local confidential analysis. Sapling's policy says submitted content can be stored and used to improve its service. Only the user-authorized nonpersonal report excerpt will be submitted; no raw data, correspondence, cover identifiers or authentication details are included. No zero-retention guarantee is asserted.
