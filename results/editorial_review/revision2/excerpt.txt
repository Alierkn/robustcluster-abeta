Radius of gyration and solvent-accessible surface area (SASA) describe related aspects of peptide shape. Their correlation, however, does not determine whether one can be omitted from a clustering model. This internship report investigates that distinction in amyloid-β40 descriptors and audits the analysis supplied for reproducing the workflow of Cadenelli and colleagues.

The dataset contains 300,003 observations in three replica segments, covering the source-labelled 1–3 microsecond interval. Inspection of the original script identified problems with parsing, residual clusters, free-energy interpolation, replica pooling and weighted summaries. These were addressed in a modular Python implementation with numerical tests and recorded configurations. A recovered free-energy surface passed grid-completeness and query checks. Its energy units and construction provenance remain unresolved, so the reported experiments use the biased descriptors without equilibrium reweighting.
