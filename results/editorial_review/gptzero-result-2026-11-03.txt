							50 container The analysis of intrinsically disordered peptides requires a distinction between the configurations saved by a simulation, their equilibrium statistical weights, and the choices used to group them. This internship report develops RobustCluster-Aβ to examine these questions using amyloid-β40 descriptors supplied for reproduction of the workflow of Cadenelli and colleagues. The published approach combines well-tempered metadynamics, density-peak clustering, Wasserstein consensus, final assignment and statistical reweighting. The present project separates that reproduction target from corrections to the supplied script and an independent analysis of feature sensitivity.




The descriptor table contains 300,003 observations in three replica segments, each covering the source-labelled 1–3 microsecond interval. An audit identified problems involving parsing, residual clusters, free-energy interpolation, replica pooling and weighted summaries. The modular implementation preserves the original inputs and adds numerical tests, parameter files, input hashes and run manifests. A subsequently recovered free-energy surface passed grid-completeness and interpolation checks for every observation; its energy-unit and construction provenance remain unresolved.
